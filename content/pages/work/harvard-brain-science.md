With Fluor, I served as the owner's representative for project controls on Harvard's Northwest Science Building in Cambridge, a multidisciplinary research facility with laboratory, classroom and office space. It houses neuroscience programs including the Center for Brain Science.

**Role — Owner's Representative, Project Controls.** Schedule tracking and alignment across stakeholders, cost control and forecasting, coordination between design, construction and the researchers who would use the building, and support for systems integration in active lab environments.

**What made it hard.** High-performance MEP systems, specialized research equipment, program changes driven by research needs, and many stakeholders with competing priorities, all inside a requirement that labs stay reconfigurable for decades.
