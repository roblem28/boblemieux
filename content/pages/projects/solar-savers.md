Solar Savers is a first-person 3D space shooter built in Three.js. Fly against waves of enemy fighters across four difficulty tiers, with a customizable HUD, weapon and core upgrades, a targeting radar, and manual pause. Runs entirely in the browser with touch and keyboard controls, no install and no plugins.
