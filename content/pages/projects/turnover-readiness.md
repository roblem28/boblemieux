**The problem.** A large capital project carries thousands of completion items across mechanical, electrical, instrumentation and controls. Tracked in separate spreadsheets, nobody sees the whole picture, and blockers surface when commissioning is already waiting.

**What I built.** A single dashboard that pulls punch list data, system completion status and handover documentation together. It flags incomplete paths and shows superintendents and commissioning teams exactly what is holding up each system.

**What changed.** Hundreds of hours of manual reconciliation went away, and handover issues surfaced weeks earlier than the spreadsheet process allowed.
