WPop maps human settlement in 3D. More than 135,000 cities rise as instanced bars from a topographic Earth, with height and color scaled by population. Filter by minimum population and geographic density, tune bar diameter, height and gamma, switch between population, heatmap and high-contrast palettes or pick custom colors, and hover any bar for the city, country and count. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and upload your own CSV with column mapping to plot any lat/lon dataset. The globe renders before the data finishes loading, and parsing runs in a Web Worker. Built with Three.js and Vite.
