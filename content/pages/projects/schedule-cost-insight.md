**The problem.** Finding what matters in a dense schedule file and a stack of cost summaries takes an experienced reader days, and early warning signs still get missed.

**What I built.** A pipeline that ingests P6 XML exports, cost reports and turnover logs, classifies activities, extracts critical path changes, summarizes variances and flags emerging risks in plain language.

**What changed.** Teams get the same read in minutes instead of days, early enough to act on cost overruns, slippage and resource conflicts before they compound.
